# Executive Scorecard

The Executive Scorecard summarizes organization-wide governance health for executive review.

## Dashboard Domains

The scorecard includes:

- Organization Health
- Department Health
- Funding Health
- Legal Health
- Knowledge Health
- AI Workforce Health
- Connector Health
- Plugin Health
- Memory Health
- Automation Health

## Operating Rhythm

The Chief of Staff can assemble these artifacts automatically from governed signals:

- Morning Briefing
- Lunch Update
- Evening Debrief
- Weekly Executive Review
- Monthly Executive Review
- Quarterly Strategy Review
- Annual Organizational Review
- Board Packet
- Grant Review
- Sponsor Review
- Department Review

Each artifact should include KPIs, risks, decisions, approvals, and evidence. The rhythm prepares executive decisions but does not authorize external action.

## Workload Balancing

The scorecard feeds Chief of Staff workload balancing for queue rebalancing, reassignment recommendations, delay escalation, bottleneck identification, staffing recommendations, and automation recommendations.
