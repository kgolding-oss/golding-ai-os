# The Law Library Funding OS

The Law Library Funding OS is a dedicated funding system inside GAIOS for coordinated revenue development, campaign execution, and human-approved funding operations.

## Dedicated agents

- **Grant Research Agent** discovers funders, scores eligibility, and tracks grant deadlines.
- **Grant Drafting Agent** drafts LOIs, proposals, narratives, budgets, and submission packages.
- **Sponsor Acquisition Agent** manages sponsor targets, sponsor packages, commitments, and deliverables.
- **Donor Development Agent** manages donor segmentation, ask drafts, stewardship, and pledge risk.
- **Partnership Development Agent** develops institutional partnerships, MOUs, and program value exchange.
- **Compliance Review Agent** reviews restricted funds, legal/nonprofit obligations, claims, and sensitive actions.
- **Impact Reporting Agent** manages outcomes, reporting calendars, funder reports, and evidence packets.
- **Campaign Operations Agent** coordinates the operating cadence, approval queue, launch checklist, and cross-agent blockers.

## GAIOS integrations

The system integrates with the Grant Development Agent, CRM Agent, Finance & Operations Agent, Research & Intelligence Agent, Media & Communications Agent, Chief of Staff, and Executive Command Agent. These integrations synchronize opportunities, relationships, financial commitments, research evidence, communications drafts, executive blockers, approvals, and command metrics.

## Tracked operating records

The runtime tracks funding goals, grants, sponsors, donors, partners, deadlines, proposals, reports, outreach drafts, approvals, weighted forecast, and risk flags.

## Human approval requirements

The system is designed as a planning and drafting layer only. Human approval is required before any external outreach, submission, sponsorship commitment, financial promise, or compliance-sensitive action is executed.

## Milestone 10 production activation

Funding Operations now includes grant pipeline, grant calendar, grant writer workspace, sponsor CRM, donor CRM, foundation CRM, corporate partnership CRM, campaign tracker, funding forecast, revenue dashboard, board reporting, impact reporting, compliance checklist, and submission queue. Grant submissions, sponsor outreach, emails, and financial transactions require explicit approval.
