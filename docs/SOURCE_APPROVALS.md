# Source Approvals

Every onboarding decision must produce an audit event that records the source, actor, action, decision, timestamp, and notes. Approval is required before GAIOS performs any of these actions:

1. Indexing file contents.
2. Importing contacts.
3. Importing emails.
4. Importing calendar events.
5. Linking records to people, cases, grants, or other operational entities.
6. Exposing records to AI agents.

Approvals are scoped to the source, workspace, classification, and operation. A metadata-only approval does not imply content indexing approval. A relationship-linking approval does not imply AI-agent exposure approval.

## Workspace mapping

Live data must be mapped to one of: The Law Library, Golding Compound, Relax With Me, YouPassGo, TLC Creations, Musa Links, YardYank, J&J Catering, or Personal Vault.
