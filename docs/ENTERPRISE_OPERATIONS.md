# Enterprise Operations & Autonomous Execution

Milestone 12 turns GAIOS into an enterprise AI operating system for day-to-day organizational work while preserving deterministic execution, auditability, and mandatory human approval for high-risk actions.

## Workspaces

Supported workspaces:

- The Law Library
- Golding Compound
- Relax With Me
- YouPassGo
- TLC Creations
- Musa Links
- YardYank
- J&J Catering

Every workspace supports Organizations, Projects, Tasks, Documents, Knowledge, CRM, Finance, Reporting, Users, and Permissions through organization-scoped records.

## Executive reports

Executive Intelligence produces these synthesis views:

- Morning Briefing
- Evening Debrief
- Weekly Executive Report
- Monthly Strategic Report
- Quarterly Board Report
- Annual Organizational Review

Each report summarizes active work, pending approvals, production queues, risks, and recommended decisions.

## Collaboration paths

- Research → Grant Writing
- Research → Legal
- CRM → Funding
- Funding → Finance
- Media → Education
- Knowledge → Legal
- Property → Finance

## Knowledge graph entities

The connected knowledge layer supports People, Organizations, Cases, Grants, Sponsors, Donors, Projects, Meetings, Documents, Programs, Tasks, and Relationships.

## Reusable workflows

The workflow engine registers approval-gated workflows for grant lifecycle, sponsor lifecycle, case lifecycle, volunteer onboarding, board meetings, project approvals, media production, and course publishing. External and high-risk actions remain blocked until a human approval record authorizes execution.

## Analytics domains

Dashboards synthesize KPI domains for Funding, Cases, Education, Partnerships, Media, Finance, Operations, Property, and AI Workforce.

## AI workforce management

AI workforce views expose agent health, delegation activity, work queues, completed activity, recommendations, pending approvals, and execution history.
