# AI Workforce Governance

AI Workforce Governance makes every agent, department, workflow, and plugin measurable and accountable.

## Explainability Standard

Every recommendation must answer:

- Why?
- What evidence?
- What policy?
- What knowledge?
- What confidence?
- What alternatives?

The decision engine records supporting evidence, knowledge sources, confidence, risks, alternatives, required approvals, expected impact, and relevant policies.

## Organizational Memory

Governance memory tracks:

- Decisions
- Policies
- Exceptions
- Board Votes
- Meeting Outcomes
- Lessons Learned
- Historical KPIs
- Historical Risks
- Strategic Objectives

## Autonomous Executive Office Boundary

The Chief of Staff may rebalance queues, recommend reassignment, escalate delays, identify bottlenecks, and recommend staffing or automation. It delegates and prepares executive decisions; it does not bypass approvals or execute unauthorized external actions.
